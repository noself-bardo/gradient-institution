## Operating rules

- Read the project-local current-state record before acting on a phase ledger or old root identity.
- Never infer authorization from the existence of assets, approvals from an earlier phase, or chat enthusiasm.
- Production uses the frozen contract until a separately tested replacement is formally promoted.
- Routine crop, font, link, archive, manifest, and packaging failures are production exceptions. They do not reopen architecture.
- Publication, release, canon admission, and archive admission require explicit authority.
- Unlisted repository areas are outside the scope of the 2026-07-31 reconciliation. Their absence from this index grants no authority and changes no state.
