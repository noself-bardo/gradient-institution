# Human Gates v1.0

`CONCEPT_APPROVED` permits editorial and specification work. It does not permit rendering.

`APPROVED_FOR_RENDER` must identify the exact volume, issue or page scope, frozen input identity, authorizing person, date, and any bounded exception. Missing, ambiguous, expired, or mismatched authorization is closed.

Founder review after assembly determines whether the proof may become a golden reference. Automated success never substitutes for this ruling.
