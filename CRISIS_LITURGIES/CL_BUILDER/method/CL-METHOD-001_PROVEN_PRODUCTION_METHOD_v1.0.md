## Proven operating contract

1. A frozen issue specification defines four page functions in invariant order: RELIC_ENTRY, WITNESS_RECORD, SYSTEM_TRANSLATION, ARCHIVE_DISPOSITION.
2. The renderer receives visual-only instructions. It does not own authoritative headers, titles, body prose, citations, folios, or final page geometry.
3. Matte black is the environmental field. The active evidence family is metallic silver, silver-white, and graphite-dark silver.
4. One governing relation organizes each page.
5. Human presence is partial, dignified, non-identifiable, non-cinematic, and subordinate to evidence.
6. Interfaces remain material objects or reflective fields, never characters.
7. Negative space carries delay, silence, hierarchy, and institutional distance.
8. Deterministic layout owns typography, coordinates, margins, overflow, page order, and PDF assembly.
9. Automated QC checks dimensions, black coverage, white-field contamination, edge behavior, transparency, foreground density, and chromatic drift.
10. Repair is page-bounded. Accepted pages are preserved; only the failed page is patched or rerendered.
11. Every accepted output receives a checksum, manifest entry, state record, and evidence trail.
12. Founder interaction is concentrated at bounded authorization and completed issue-proof review.

## Evidence

MIRROR proved a four-page issue with one detected conceptual contamination and one bounded Page 4 repair. IVORY proved transfer to a materially different issue with four first-pass accepted plates and no rerenders. Together they establish a transferable method, not a universal visual template.
